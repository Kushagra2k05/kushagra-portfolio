RevenueOS

lang: Python · type: AI / fintech
AI-powered revenue recovery for failed payments
  • For each failed payment, estimates why it failed, whether and when to retry, and how much revenue the retries recover — the headline metric is incremental revenue recovered.
  • Pairs two ML models (an XGBoost failure classifier and a calibrated recovery-probability model, ROC-AUC 0.75) with a deterministic policy engine that turns predictions into actions, each with an audit trail.
  • Ships a live Next.js dashboard — transactions, analytics, A/B experiments on retry timing, an AI merchant copilot, and a business-impact calculator — backed by FastAPI, Celery/Redis async retries, JWT auth, and SHAP-based explanations.
  • Built on a synthetic pipeline of 100,000 simulated transactions; 34 passing pytest tests cover the policy and ML logic.
